Mob difficulty curve — how many hits to kill a mob

Hits required to fell a reference minion (its hp ÷ the hero's landed blow after mob armor), across hero level. The hero is geared by the analytic progression sim. A rising line means mobs demand more hits as the game goes on; a line pinned near the bottom means the hero one-shots the horde (which pins the rampage meter at its cap). Dashed line = the ~10-hits target.
