---
type: Added
title: A weapon carries its own signature look
---

A named weapon's signature slash, muzzle flash and gore are now authored beside
its numbers (`fx:` in the item's own YAML) rather than in a table keyed by
shipped ids — so a mod's legendary flares its own element instead of swinging
the plain class look. Every element reads the same on a blade and on a gun, and
the arsenal pages name each relic's signature.
