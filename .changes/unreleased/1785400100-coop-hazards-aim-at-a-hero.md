title: Co-op — the weather falls on somebody, not on the middle of the party
---

In a multiplayer run the meteor rain, the sand storms, the employee stampedes
and the rolling hay were all aimed at the party's geometric middle. A blast
bills every hero in range, so a group that stayed together was caught by every
single rock — while a group that spread out had the weather fall on empty floor
between them and stop existing. Hazards now pick a hero and fall on them, so the
rate each player faces is the one a solo run has always had and standing
shoulder to shoulder means sharing a splash rather than multiplying it.

Single player is unaffected.
