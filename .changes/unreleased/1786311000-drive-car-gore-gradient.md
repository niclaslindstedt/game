type: Changed
title: Blood on the car spreads
---

The wagon no longer arrives at the first crossing with a drenched bumper bolted to a showroom-fresh body: what lands on the nose is now dragged back over the bonnet, the glass and the flanks by the airstream, so the car reads as filthy at the front and grubby at the tail. The blood film itself is redrawn as a real ladder — sparse spatter with the paint plainly showing, then wet patches, then total coverage — and a panel can no longer come out looking cleaner than the one beside it.
