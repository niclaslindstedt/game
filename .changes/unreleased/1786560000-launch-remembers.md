title: The launch remembers what it did to the house
---

Lift off the lawn a second time and the house is the one you left: the garage end blackened, its roof burnt through, the siding dusty. The blast blackens what is already black and the roof catches again — nothing gets worse, it just never gets better, and nobody ever mentions it. The lawn keeps its grass now too, except for the dead patch the ship actually stands on — the same patch you walk around in the garage.
