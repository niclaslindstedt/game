title: Why he goes to GOODCO
---

Ada's beacon answers from GOODCO rather than from space, so the first mission is a man driving out to bring his girlfriend home from the place he used to work — not an errand to fetch a part that happens to be in the same building she vanished into. He gets there an hour late; she has already been flown off the planet, and the part his own ship has always been short of turns out to be the only way to follow her.
