type: Changed
title: Every number on the character sheet now says where it came from
---

Hover a character-sheet row on a desktop, or tap it on a phone, and it explains
itself: what the figure means, and — for the five attributes — the split between
the points you chose, the difficulty's head start, the automatic growth each
level hands out, and gear. So a hero who has spent nothing and still reads
1 INTELLECT now says which carried charm put it there instead of looking like a
bug. ARMOR is two rows rather than `6 (-6%)`, which read as a penalty to
something: the points stand on their own and DMG REDUCE states the share of a
blow they turn against the horde you are actually fighting. The bag's purse and
ammunition sockets answer the same way — what the kind is, how deep the stack
goes, and which of your weapons fires it — a passive charm's item card finally
prints the bonus it pays from your pocket, and the level-up chooser's (i) panel
says out loud that its numbers are your own picks and nothing else.
