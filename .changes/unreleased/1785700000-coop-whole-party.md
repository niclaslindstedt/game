title: A joiner is a first-class player
---

Co-op grew its whole R1 slate: the party is visible (every hero in play is
drawn on the field, with party frames on the HUD showing health, level, name
and screen state), a session survives travel (the host picks a door and the
whole party crosses together — joiners are no longer dropped at every level
swap), a joiner brings their OWN character and banks everything they earn back
to their own roster, the trade window is a real screen (offer, coins, both
READY lamps, tap a teammate's party frame or the pause roster to open one),
the host's mod set applies to a joiner who has the mods installed (and a
missing mod points at the Workshop), and the garage's workbenches open the
LOST & FOUND — the stash finally has a place.
