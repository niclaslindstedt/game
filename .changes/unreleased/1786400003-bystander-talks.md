title: Bystanders say their piece and let you go
---

The three people on the campaign chain who used to hold you in a branching conversation — the archive unit, the moon's site surveyor and the tithe assessor — now say one thing and take one answer, like everybody else. The surveyor in particular could be lost for good by opening with the wrong line, which quietly dead-ended a campaign errand for the rest of that run with nothing to say why.
