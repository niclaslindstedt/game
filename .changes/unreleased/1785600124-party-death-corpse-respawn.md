---
type: Added
title: Death is per-player in co-op
---

A hero who falls while the party still stands now goes down instead of ending the run: they pay their own XP toll, leave a corpse holding their worn gear where they fell (only its owner can recover it, and gear never left behind is banked into the LOST & FOUND at level end), and a RESPAWN button stands them back up at the level's start at full health for the walk back. Hardcore and softcore heroes can no longer share a session — the handshake refuses the mix by name. Solo play is unchanged: your death still ends the run exactly as before.
