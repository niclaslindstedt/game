---
type: Added
title: Bosses kneel before they die
---

Every boss now has an authored pose for the stagger beat of its death rite, so it visibly gives way before the hero finishes it: ELON MOSQUE drops onto both knees with his head bowed, ARMSTRONG stops hovering level and settles onto his own hem, THE ZAI SUPERCORE cants over on its wheels, and the machines buckle down onto their frames.
