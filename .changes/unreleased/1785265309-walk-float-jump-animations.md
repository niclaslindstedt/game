type: Added
title: Walk, float and jump animations
---

Everything on the field now carries itself: bodies on legs tip softly left and
right and rise on each step — faster and harder the faster they actually move —
ghosts and other legless monsters hover over a shadow instead, and a jump gets a
takeoff stretch, a landing squash, and a puff of dust and gravel in the colour of
the ground it was kicked off.
