---
type: Changed
title: Desktop opens on AIM & SHOOT, and the hero picks the best target
---

A desktop mouse now steers **AIM & SHOOT** out of the box — WASD walks, the
pointer aims, the left button fires — instead of cursor-follow. FOLLOW CURSOR is
still one press away under SETTINGS → CONTROLS, and touch is unchanged.

**FOLLOW CURSOR no longer aims with the cursor.** The same pixel was being read
as both "walk here" and "shoot that", so running past a boss switched the hero's
fire onto whichever minion happened to lie along the walk. It — and every other
scheme with nobody pointing — now picks the **best target**: still the nearest
foe in reach, but weighted by role, so an elite outranks a minion up to twice as
close and a boss one up to four times as close. It stays a preference rather
than a lock: a minion right on top of the hero still takes the blow, and in
AIM & SHOOT the cursor still outranks the weighting.
