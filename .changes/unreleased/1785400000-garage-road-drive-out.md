title: Drive out to the road — the garage's departure is a scene, not a cut
---

The garage now has a ROAD down its far east edge: two lanes of tarmac with a
painted centre line and a grass verge, running off both ends of the lot.

Leaving for GOODCO HQ is a drive rather than a cut. The roll-up still opens as
you pull up to it, but the trip no longer commits on the threshold — you drive
out of the bay, across the driveway and onto the tarmac, and it is the road that
ends the level. Reaching it hands the wheel over: the car swings into the
right-hand lane and drives away up the road while the whole picture — field,
HUD and all — fades to black behind it.
