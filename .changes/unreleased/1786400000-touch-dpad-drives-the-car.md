title: The touch dpad drives the car
---

On touch, the virtual dpad now points where the car should go on the screen: a
push along the nose drives (or reverses), a push up or down curves the moving
car up or down the screen, and a pure vertical push carries no throttle — a
parked car stays parked. Holding a diagonal no longer sends the car around in
an endless circle, and a car left rolling with a sideways push held now coasts
to a stop instead of creeping forever.
