type: Added
title: Cleaved in two, or burst into pieces — what an overwhelming blow leaves
---

A killing blow far past what a body could hold now takes that body APART, and
the weapon decides how. An **edged** melee weapon goes THROUGH, and **never twice
the same way**: the cut is rolled fresh on every kill, and what falls out of it
is whatever it went through. It beheads and the skull comes with it. It opens a
chest and the ribcage and the heart come out. It disembowels. It cuts a man in
half at the waist, or takes his legs at the knees and leaves them standing where
they were, or splits him crown to crotch and empties him. Where you were
standing, how hard you hit and the kill's own luck decide which — so the same
sword on the same monster keeps showing you something you have not seen.

Anything **blunt** — a maul, a round, a spell, a bomb, a hazard — BURSTS it
instead, throwing meat, intestines, a ribcage, a heart, a liver, a kidney, a skull, a brain and bone
across the floor, along with fragments of the monster's own sprite so a burst
looks like the thing it burst.

The pieces fly on the same arc a dropped item does, shadow and all, and land on
the blood they spilled getting there. What BOUNCES is what it is made of: a
skull, a ribcage, a kidney and a heart kick back off the floor and skitter; a
liver, a gut and a slab of meat land once and stay in the puddle they made.

The pieces lie where they landed for ten seconds, so a room you cleared still
looks cleared when you walk back through it (DEVELOPER → VISUALS → GORE LINGER
dials it).

It has to be EARNED — an ordinary kill still punts the body and topples it — and
a boss never comes apart. Melee weapons say which they are (`edge: blunt` on the
hammers, batons and knuckles) and monsters say what they are built of
(`anatomy: beast` for the ones with no face to lose), both authorable by a mod.

All of it hangs off the existing MATURE CONTENT device switch and the player's
own SETTINGS → DISPLAY → EXTRA GORE row; with either off, an overwhelming blow
lands exactly as it always did. See the new CLEAVED IN TWO and BURST INTO PIECES
exhibits in the developer effects gallery.
