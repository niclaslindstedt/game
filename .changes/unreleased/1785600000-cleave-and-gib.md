type: Added
title: Cleaved in two, or burst into pieces — what an overwhelming blow leaves
---

A killing blow far past what a body could hold now takes that body APART, and
the weapon decides how. An **edged** melee weapon goes THROUGH: the monster's own
sprite is cut in two along the swing and the halves keel outward, the open cut
between them and a length of gut hanging out of it. Anything **blunt** — a
maul, a round, a spell, a bomb, a hazard — BURSTS it instead, throwing meat,
intestines, a ribcage, an arm, a hand, a foot, a heart, a liver, a kidney, bone
and a ruined head across the floor, along with fragments of the monster's own
art so a burst looks like the thing it burst.

The pieces fly on the same arc a dropped item does, shadow and all, and land on
the blood they spilled getting there. What BOUNCES is what it is made of: a
skull, a ribcage, a kidney and a heart kick back off the floor and skitter; a
liver, a gut and a slab of meat land once and stay in the puddle they made.

It has to be EARNED — an ordinary kill still punts the body and topples it — and
a boss never comes apart. Melee weapons say which they are (`edge: blunt` on the
hammers, batons and knuckles) and monsters say what they are built of
(`anatomy: beast` for the ones with no face to lose), both authorable by a mod.

All of it hangs off the existing MATURE CONTENT device switch and the player's
own SETTINGS → DISPLAY → EXTRA GORE row; with either off, an overwhelming blow
lands exactly as it always did. See the new CLEAVED IN TWO and BURST INTO PIECES
exhibits in the developer effects gallery.
