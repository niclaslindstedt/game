title: THE GARAGE — home base
---

The campaign now opens at HOME: the hero's garage is a hub level — the one
static map in the game — with the merchant parked at his counter (buying loot
and repairing gear), a stocked workbench world to walk, and three ways out:
the car to GOODCO HQ, the rocket to the moon and Mars, and a rift seam that
stays SEALED until THE FOUNDER's RIFT CREATOR comes home from the rift —
after which every earthside victory loops back home, Diablo-style. Its own
sanctuary theme plays there.
