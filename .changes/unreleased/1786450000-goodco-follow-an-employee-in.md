title: Follow the night shift into GOODCO
---

GOODCO's staff lot is a car park again rather than a brawl: nothing ambient stands on it, just two parking guards who are not looking for anybody. The way into the building is now something you have to find — every so often a car pulls in off the road, somebody gets out and walks to the entrance, and taps a card against it. The doors only ever open for that card, so you go in behind them, and the floor's first swing at you lands inside where the desks are.
