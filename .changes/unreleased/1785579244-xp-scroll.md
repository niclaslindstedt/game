---
type: Changed
title: XP scrolls replace golden arrows
---

The golden XP arrow is gone. In its place the horde drops **XP SCROLLS**, which
read themselves the moment you walk over one: for the next 30 seconds every
scrap of experience you earn counts **double**, and a faint blue veil around
your character says the clock is running. A scroll pays nothing on its own, so
what one is worth is whatever you do with those thirty seconds — read one into a
pack and it pays for itself, read one over a cleared floor and it doubles
nothing. A second scroll REFRESHES the window rather than stacking it, so a body
never sheds more than one. Drop rates are trimmed to match: measured over a full
campaign, the scroll pays the same share of your XP the arrow did, so levelling
lands exactly where it did before.
