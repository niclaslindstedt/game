title: A whole town on the road to GOODCO
---

The buildings on the drive were rebuilt from scratch. There are 26 kinds of them now instead of eight, in three colourways each, and no two are the same size: terraces, tenements, lock-ups, a chippy, a pub, a chapel, a scrapyard, a showroom and an office block, from 16 px of gated alley to a five-plot depot, and from a single-storey kiosk to a four-storey block. Each one is dressed where it stands — its own door, windows, shopfront, awning, sign, fence and the junk in its front garden — so the same house twice in a row is never the same picture twice.

And the street changes as you drive it. The road out of the hero's block is boarded windows, smashed glass, graffiti, broken fences and sofas in front gardens; the last mile before GOODCO is clean render, glass frontages, clipped hedges and lit offices. Nothing announces where it changes.
