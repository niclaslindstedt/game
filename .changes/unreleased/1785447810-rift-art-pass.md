---
type: Changed
title: THE RIFT's worst-reading art redrawn
---

Six sprites in THE RIFT were redrawn to match what their own definitions already
described: the unraveler now comes apart instead of being a speckled blob, the
last photon is the starburst it was written as, the stray comet has its facets
and its tail, the rift shard is faceted crystal rather than a rounded crown, the
voidling's eyes read as eyes, and the collapsed star shows the event horizon
that tells it apart from the star jelly it fell from.
