---
type: Changed
title: THE RIFT's worst-reading art redrawn
---

Seven sprites in THE RIFT were redrawn to match what their own definitions
already described: the unraveler now comes apart instead of being a speckled
blob, the last photon is the starburst it was written as, the stray comet has
its facets and its tail, the rift shard is faceted crystal rather than a rounded
crown, the voidling's eyes read as eyes, the collapsed star shows its event
horizon, and THE KING wears the gold-trimmed collar and sunburst his own
description promised.
