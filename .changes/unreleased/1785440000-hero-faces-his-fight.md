title: The hero faces what he is fighting, not where his legs go
---

A hero shooting to his left while running right is now mirrored onto the
FIGHT — he reads as running backward, which is what he is doing. Everything he
holds is drawn inside that mirror, so the held weapon, its swing arc and its
muzzle flash all stayed behind him whenever he fought something he was not
walking toward: on touch whenever the auto-aim picked a foe behind him, and on
desktop AIM & SHOOT whenever the cursor was held opposite the WASD steer.

He keeps the fighting facing for as long as the weapon is recovering from the
blow, so he holds his guard on the pack between shots instead of snapping back
to his legs; out of a fight, his legs have him again as before. With AIM &
SHOOT he also turns with the cursor the instant it crosses him, trigger down or
not.
