title: Multiplayer sessions survive a busy host, a departure and a shop visit
---

A crash when the autopilot sized up the merchant's stall in a session somebody
else was hosting. Joining a session several people arrive at once now waits its
turn instead of reporting that the session stopped answering, and a session no
longer freezes for everybody when the player who owed a level-up quits or goes
down.
