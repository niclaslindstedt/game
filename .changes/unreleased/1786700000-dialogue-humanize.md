title: The script sounds like people talking
---

A pass over every voice in the campaign, trading the lines that read like a film trailer for the way somebody would actually say them. GOODCO is no longer "an AI learned my job and they walked me out" — they sat a machine at his bench and gave him a box for his mug. The scientist on the HQ floor now begs before he swings, the hero answers "STAY BACK. PLEASE. I'VE NEVER HIT ANYBODY IN MY LIFE. DON'T.", Ruth asks a favour like a mother-in-law rather than a herald, and the thesis statements over a dying boss are gone.
