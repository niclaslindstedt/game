---
type: Changed
title: The merchant's counter — a hover card, a consumable shelf, and a stall that runs out
---

Tapping something at the merchant now raises a **floating card** beside it with
the trade's own BUY/SELL button in it, instead of filling a fixed panel at the
foot of the shop — which used to reserve room for the tallest item it might ever
show, on every frame, including the ones where nothing was selected. The counter
is a good deal smaller for it, and a weapon on the stall reads through the exact
same item card the inventory floats.

He also **sells medkits, repair kits and energy drinks** now — a few of each,
banked into the same dock stacks a floor pickup would, so the counter is the one
place a hero can choose to be stocked before a boss instead of hoping the rain
pays a kit.

And **what he sells is what he sells**: the stall is rolled once, at the meeting,
and nothing restocks. Every row shows how many are left and sells out when they
are gone; the powerups used to restock forever, which made standing at the
counter a better source of powers than the whole drop ladder.
