title: The field no longer ripples as you walk
---

Everything standing on the floor — the horde, the props, the loot, the
corpses — used to shift a pixel against each other and against the ground
whenever the hero moved north or south, while east-west travel stayed rock
solid. Bodies now sit on the same whole-pixel lattice the baked floor and the
fog register against, so the picture stays rigid at any camera pitch or yaw.
