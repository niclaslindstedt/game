type: Fixed
title: Bloom stopped glowing the floor, and now ships off
---

SETTINGS → VISUALS → **BLOOM** was blooming the ground rather than the lights.
Its "what counts as light" threshold sat at 0.49 luminance while the moon's
regolith is 0.554 and GOODCO HQ's deck 0.701 — so most of every frame was
classed as a light source and added back over itself, lifting the whole
picture's brightness by 14–24% and leaving the moon a milky lavender and the HQ
deck bleached. It also reached its blur buffer in one quarter-size minify, which
Canvas2D undersamples badly enough to drop a light entirely depending on where
it sits, so the halo pulsed as the camera panned — measured, up to 1.9 luminance
points of jump between consecutive one-pixel pans, now 0.14.

The threshold now sits above every floor in the game, the downscale is two exact
halvings, and the effect is dialled back to a halo a light can wear without
losing its drawing. **It now ships OFF**: on pixel art this small, every
luminance point a halo adds is one of the artist's own shading it paints over,
so it is offered rather than assumed — turn it on under SETTINGS → VISUALS.
