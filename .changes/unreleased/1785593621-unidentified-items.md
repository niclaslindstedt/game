title: Unidentified items & the merchant's appraisal
---

Magic-or-better finds now drop unidentified, Diablo 2 style: the piece shows only its base name in its rarity color and cannot be worn until it is identified — at the merchant's new IDENTIFY counter service, or in the field with an ITEM LOOKUP TICKET from his stall (they stack twenty to a bag cell). The item-found reveal now plays at the moment of identification, center stage, with the piece's full stats.
