title: The launch actually looks like a launch
---

The garage cutscene now plays against the same night sky as the drive — the same moon, stars, clouds and open country along the horizon — hung still, with only the clouds drifting; and when the camera follows the ship up, every layer falls away at its own distance instead of the backdrop sliding as one piece. The rocket got the engine to go with it: a plume twice the length of the hull, and a wall of fire and smoke that runs the length of the lot when it lights. It is parked much closer to the house now, which the house notices — the garage side goes black and the roof catches before the ship is clear. The lawn is charred earth from then on, in the cutscene and in the hub you walk back into.
