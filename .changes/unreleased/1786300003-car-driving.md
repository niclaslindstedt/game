title: The car drives like a car
---

Real driving controls: W accelerates along the nose, S brakes and then backs
up, A and D steer the rolling car (a parked one cannot pivot). The body now
collides with walls and furniture, and driving out of the garage means rolling
the garage door up and crossing its threshold — the trip to GOODCO commits at
the doorway, not at an invisible distance line.
