title: Ghosts, machines and rift-things come apart too — and every hit throws a cloud
---

Only people bled. A ghost, a rover and a thing out of the rift kept the same
plain two-frame splash they were killed with whatever hit them, and none of them
could be cut in two or burst — which made three quarters of the roster the one
part of the game a blow did not really land on.

Now **every kind of body comes apart, and each comes apart as itself.**

A **HAUNTING** opens on green goo and a cold light: a veil, a ring, a mask, a
shard, and the ember that was its face. It leaves that goo on the floor, in
green, for the rest of the level.

A **MACHINE** opens on plate over a loom of wire, with cells, servos, pistons and
a still-lit optic in it, and oil through the lot. Everything a machine is made of
BOUNCES except its oil — the exact inverse of a body — and the oil stays on the
deck. It smokes where it stood, and the smoke climbs and outlives the burst that
made it.

A **RIFT-THING** opens on shards, motes, threads and a collapsed heart. Only that
heart has any weight; light does not bounce. It glimmers where the body was, and
then the floor is clean, because light goes out.

The cut rule is untouched, which is the point: a cut still spills **what it went
through**, so one across a rover's head spills its eye for exactly the reason one
across a man's neck spills his skull.

**And every landed blow now throws a CLOUD** — a wash of the victim's own colour
that blooms at the wound and hangs there, so a hit reads before a single drop has
travelled anywhere. Red out of a person, green out of a ghost, oily black out of
a machine, violet out of a rift-thing. It is a wash the fight is seen through,
never a curtain over it.

All of it hangs off the same MATURE CONTENT device switch and SETTINGS → DISPLAY
→ EXTRA GORE row the rest of the gore does; with either off, every family lands
exactly as it always did. Seven new exhibits in the developer effects gallery
(A HAUNTING / A MACHINE / A RIFT-THING COMES APART, and four that pin the rare
cuts so they can actually be looked at: TAKEN AT THE NECK, WALKED OUT FROM UNDER
HIMSELF, SLICED THROUGH THE DEPTH, A SLAB OFF THE FRONT).
