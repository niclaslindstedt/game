title: The rocket's trash, the orbit beat, and MOON LANDING
---

Space trash now bounces off the rocket by its own weight — scuffing the hull and shoving the balance instead of sticking — and reaching orbit plays out in full: the ship settles, floats, drops its booster and flies on, two new cabin scenes look back at Earth and ahead to the moon, a MOON LANDING briefing hands over the drop, and the landing is playable on its own from the minigames shelf.
