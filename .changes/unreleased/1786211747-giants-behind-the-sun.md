type: Changed
title: The giants keep to their own side of the sun
---

The title screen's viewpoint now stands between Mars and Jupiter, so Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune never come round in front of the star: each swings into frame from one side, crosses behind the sun lit and small, and leaves by the other. Their orbits are also spaced far enough apart to stop the outer four reading as one crowded ring.
