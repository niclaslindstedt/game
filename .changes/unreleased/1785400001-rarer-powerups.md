type: Changed
title: Powerups are rarer — and the run-saving ones are rarer still
---

A powerup is a moment, not a resource. Powers now fall about half as often, and
which power a drop pays is weighted by how big a deal it is: the classics turn up
as they always did, while a REACTOR SURGE, an EVENT HORIZON or a CONTINUITY
PROTOCOL is a genuine find. The merchant reads the same weights — a rare power
costs more on his counter, so coins cannot buy past the rationing.
