type: Fixed
title: Gore stays on the floor, and the blood stops wobbling
---

Corpses, gibs and cleaved bodies that have come to rest are drawn under the
cast now, so the hero walks OVER the mess he made instead of having a chunk of
somebody painted across him every time he crosses the spot. A body still in
the air — keeling over, or sailing off a killing blow — is drawn over the field
as before.

The blood on the floor and the boot prints tracked out of it also stopped
shifting against the ground as the hero walks north or diagonally: the stains
are now baked flat once and blitted on the same whole-pixel grid the floor, the
horde and the fog already share.
