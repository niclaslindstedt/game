title: The road home has its own theme
---

THE DRIVE now plays a different score in each direction. Going out is OVERDUE, unchanged in character — the clock that never stops. Coming home is AN HOUR BEHIND, and it is not the calmer one: she left the planet an hour before you got through the door, so the clock is gone, every phrase is a call with the reply cut out, and the harmony falls without ever landing. The arcade cabinet offers both.
