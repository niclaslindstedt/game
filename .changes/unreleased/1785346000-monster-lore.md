type: Added
title: Every monster in the game now says what it is
---

All 106 monsters — the fodder tier as much as the named elites and bosses —
carry a paragraph of lore saying what the thing is and how it came to be
standing there, and the library's bestiary prints it under each portrait. A
COWBOT is a decommissioned park host that used to take a bullet twice a day for
the guests; a WISP is the thinnest thing the singing wreck under the Sea of
Tranquility woke; the bunker's bodyguards are the machine's wardens wearing
each resident's livery. The rank and file are the only monsters that never get
to explain themselves in the game itself, which is why the field is required of
them too — a horde nobody wrote a line about reads as a texture rather than as
the inhabitants of somewhere.

Mod authors get the same field: `lore` is now required on a modded monster, and
`mod/FORMAT.md` documents it.
