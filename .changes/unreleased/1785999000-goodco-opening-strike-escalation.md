title: GOODCO HQ opens with three blows, not one — and he warns them first
---

The scripted opening strike is a scene now instead of a formality. The hero
walks into his old workplace with the wall piece holstered, a lab scientist he
knows breaks from the night shift and hits him — and he does not hit back. He
names the man, tells the floor to stand down, says he has never raised a hand
to anyone in his life. The scientist answers him: they have their orders,
nobody walks out of there with GOODCO's secrets, and he would like to keep his
job — as the hero, of all people, knows. He is hit a second time, and a third,
and only then does he answer one, apologising while he does it.

Each blow is its own beat: he shoves the man off, the man picks himself up and
comes back, so the escalation is something the player watches happen rather
than three monologues in a row. A replay still arms on the first blow — nobody
sits through the scene twice.

The dialogue box can now carry a second voice in one of the hero's own pinned
beats, which is what lets the scientist answer at all, and the whole exchange
is published in the library's GOODCO HQ chapter — where the scripted strike
used to appear nowhere at all.
