---
type: Changed
title: Intros replay until a level is cleared
---

A mission's opening — the prelude and the hero's intro monologue — now plays
on every fresh entry until the level is actually cleared on that difficulty;
dying or leaving partway no longer silences the story. A cleared level skips
to the level-name card as before, and the garage keeps its opening to the
first visit. Inner monologues stay read-once per difficulty.
