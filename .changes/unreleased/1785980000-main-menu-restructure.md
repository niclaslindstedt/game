---
type: Changed
title: The menus, rebuilt around what players already know
---

NEW GAME and LOAD GAME now sit on the front door itself instead of behind a PLAY
submenu, the badges, boards, buy-back and field guide moved onto a new EXTRAS
shelf, and SETTINGS became six pages named the way every other game names them —
GAMEPLAY, CONTROLS, INTERFACE, VIDEO, AUDIO and DATA — so nothing has to be
hunted for. The desktop build gained a QUIT row, a locked row no longer lights up
as though it were pressable, and every BACK now lands on the row it was opened
from on every build.
