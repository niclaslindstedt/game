title: The road's heavier hits
---

A body met square at speed on the road now gets the heavy crunch it was always
meant to — the line between the two takes sat five times too high, so on EASY
and MEDIUM nothing you could hit was ever loud enough to reach it. A car you
shunt is also held to the tarmac now instead of being flung onto the verge, and
a dead engine's smoke follows the wreck as it rolls rather than staying behind
at the spot it gave up on.
