type: Changed
title: Bodies come apart on OVERKILL now, not on the size of the blow
---

Whether a killing blow cuts a body in two or bursts it is now decided by how far
past dead it drove that body — the damage MINUS the health the thing still had —
instead of by the raw size of the number. It is QuakeWorld's rule, down to the
threshold: Quake gibs at 40 health past zero on a 100-health bar, and this gibs
at four tenths of the victim's own bar past zero.

The difference is the one you were seeing. A mob that dies in two hits and is
left on a fifth of its health takes the second blow almost entirely past dead —
the damage is far bigger than what was left standing — so it BURSTS. The same
blow landing on that mob at full health merely kills it, because nearly all of it
was spent getting to zero. Judging on the blow alone could not tell those two
apart, so it got them backwards: the honest one-shot toppled politely while the
chip finish exploded, and which death you got had no relation to what you had
just done.

So the spectacle now tracks your build instead of the horde's luck. An even
fight — three or four hits to drop something — leaves bodies whole. Outgrow the
things you are fighting and they start coming apart; outgrow them badly and
almost everything does, which is the point.
