title: OVERPRESSURE — a new powerup that puts four times behind every blow
---

A pressure line off the assembly floor, cut open and pointed the wrong way.
Nothing is conjured, nothing is thrown and nothing gets faster: for six seconds
every blow you already throw simply arrives with **four times** behind it.

What that does to the horde is the point. A blow four times what a body was
holding is far past the line where things stop dying and start coming apart, so
for those six seconds the room does not fall over — it bursts, one body per
swing. It is the dock's rarest treat alongside the death ward, it is in the pool
from the launch pad onward so any mission can turn one up, and it is deliberately
short. It is a punchline, not a phase of the fight.

Its own exhibit is in the developer effects gallery's POWERS shelf.
