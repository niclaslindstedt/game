title: A Diablo 2 paper doll, and a character sheet of your own
---

The inventory's equipment slots are laid out as a BODY instead of a row of
identical squares — a long weapon bay down the left, the bag balancing it on the
right, head/chest/legs/feet down the middle, and the amulet and rings tucked in
at the collar and hips — with each frame sized to what it holds and every empty
one ghosting the glyph of the piece it wants. The hero's numbers moved out of the
hover popover that hid under the old portrait into their own CHARACTER sheet,
opened by pressing your portrait in the HUD (the bag pouch beside it opens the
bag), showing attributes, offense, defense and body figures, the level bar and
the purse.
