---
type: Changed
title: Level-ups no longer pause the run
---

A ding now celebrates on the field and banks its points — a pip on the HUD
opens the stat chooser whenever you're ready, and it can be closed again with
points still banked. In co-op, the bag, the map, the shop, the pause menu and
every other screen are yours alone: one player in a menu no longer freezes the
party, and a hero with a screen open still stands on the field and can still
be hit. A run parked by an older version can't be resumed and restarts its
level.
