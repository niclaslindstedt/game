---
type: Changed
title: A rear-ending tears a car apart
---

Hard rear impacts in the DRIVE now crumple the struck car, tear both back wheels free, break its glass, scatter smoke and road-speed debris, and leave the wheel-less shell grinding sparks into the street. Collision sound weight follows closing speed, head-on crashes throw each occupant forward with the wreck, and restarting after a destroyed car restores the engine note.
