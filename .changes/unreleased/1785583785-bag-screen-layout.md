type: Changed
title: The bag is a Diablo 2 grid, and every window has a grain
---

The bag screen now draws its carry as one framed rectangle of cells the way
Diablo 2 does — with the cells STRENGTH and a worn bag have not unlocked yet
shown sealed rather than missing — the purse and the ammunition counts sit in
cutouts on the foot rail instead of floating loose on it, and every in-game
window trades its smooth gradient for a chiselled stone grain.
