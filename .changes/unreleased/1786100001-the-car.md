title: The car
---

The hatchback in the garage is a real machine: assembled from body panels
with four damage rungs each, breakable glass, suspension that answers bumps,
wheels that roll from speed — and parts that work loose, dangle off their
hinges, or tear away entirely (wheels bounce off down the road; a car missing
its front wheel sits nose-down). Tap it to climb in: the engine cranks and
catches, the head- and taillights come on with real thrown light, the body
shivers at idle, and the rumble rises as you drive. Driving out of the garage
takes you to GOODCO HQ.
