type: Added
title: The library now documents every talent, rank by rank
---

The library grows a sixth section: **the talents**. An index laying out the
three passive trees — WARLORD off STRENGTH, WINDRUNNER off DEXTERITY, ARCHON off
INTELLIGENCE — and the point economy behind them, plus a page for each of the 24
talents in them.

Every page states what each individual rank actually buys, not what a rank is
"worth": the chance CRIPPLING SHOT hobbles on at rank 4, the exact ring FROST
NOVA freezes at rank 2, the number of orbs ORBITING FLAMES is turning at rank 5.
The figures are read straight out of the game with the talent trained, so they
carry the ceilings, the gates and the mastery ranks the picker's one-line blurb
has no room for — and a mastered kicker that does nothing below its own rank
says so, instead of quoting a number that can never happen.

Each page also prices the talent against the build: what a maxed copy costs in
chosen stat points, whether the bonus counts only on that tree's own weapon, and
the rest of the tree the rank was bought instead of. A conjuration links across
to the pickup that puts the same thing on the field, and says what the permanent
version changes.

This is the surface the game itself has never had: the picker shows a hero a
whole tree for about as long as it takes to read one line, once per point.
