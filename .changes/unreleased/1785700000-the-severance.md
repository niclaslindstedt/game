title: THE SEVERANCE and the first respec
---

A nine-errand chain that runs across all five venues and is carried on the hero rather than on the run — starting with a clerk who needs a typewriter ribbon and ending with the book the machine files people in. It brings NEUTRAL bystanders you can talk to (and talk into swinging at you), conversations you steer by picking what to say, errands that run through the trader's counter, places to find with nothing pointing at them, and — on JESUS, at level 99 — THE BIBLE: a clean slate that refunds your whole build to be re-placed, the only respec in the game.
