---
type: Changed
title: The quest log is a button on the HUD, and progress is announced where you are looking
---

The QUEST LOG has moved out of the pause menu and onto the HUD itself: a round
`!` button beside the bag pouch, one press from the fight instead of two presses
into a screen about quitting. It is **gold** once you are carrying an errand and
**grey** until then, so the colour answers "is there anything in there" without
you having to open it. Pressing it freezes the run exactly as the map does;
ESCAPE (or CLOSE) drops you back in.

Progress on an errand is now **flashed over the middle of the field** the moment
it moves — `ASSEMBLER: 3/8` on the kill that counted — instead of only ticking
over in the corner tracker nobody is looking at during a fight. Every kind of
progress is announced: a kill off a list, a named elite going down, a fetch
piece walked over, an escort delivered; the last one of a set turns green.

The on-screen tracker (right of the field, under the minimap) also keeps up
properly now — a delivered escort or a piece picked up off the floor used to
leave it showing a stale count until some unrelated kill shook it loose.
