---
type: Added
title: Every elite fights with a kit of its own
---

All 27 named elites now carry set-piece abilities — a signature move in their
own colours plus a second shared with a few others — built out of the hero's own
vocabulary (an orbiting ring, homing bolts, a burning trail, a snare, a drain, a
shell) rather than the arena-eating set pieces the bosses use. Every mob that
telegraphs a move now wears a cast pose while it winds up, mods can author the
same abilities on their own monsters, and each one has a page in the library and
an exhibit in the effects gallery.
