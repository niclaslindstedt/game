---
type: Added
title: A mod can author the passive talent trees
---

The three passive talent trees are content now (`content/talents.yaml`), so a
mod can add a passive to the picker, retune a shipped one, or — as a total
conversion — replace the build system outright. A talent's structured procs (a
parry, a volley, a frost nova) carry their own chances and radii on the def and
are found by the effect they fire rather than by name, so a mod's talent can own
one with its own numbers. How a talent point is earned, and the rank ceiling
every talent shares, stay the game's.
