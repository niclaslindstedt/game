type: Changed
title: Everyone talks like a person
---

The script has been retoned so the cast sounds like people talking rather than lines written to be quoted: feelings are said out loud, worn phrases are allowed, and nobody saves their best line for last. Ruth misses Ada and says so, the staff in your way excuse themselves the way people always have, and every elite and boss goes out on something ordinary instead of a punchline. The machines still talk like machines.
