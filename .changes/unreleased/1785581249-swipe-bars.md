---
type: Added
title: Swipe bars
---

A new touch accessibility mode (SETTINGS → GAMEPLAY → SWIPE BARS): the item and
powerup bars leave the corners, and a swipe in from the left, right or bottom
screen edge opens both — with larger icons — right where the thumb swiped, so
one-handed players never have to reach a corner. Pressing a slot spends it and
the bar folds away.
