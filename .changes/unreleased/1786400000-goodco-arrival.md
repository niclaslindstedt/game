The drive now fades out with the wagon still rolling in past the fence instead of parking it on the road and standing the hero on the tarmac — the level opens with the car already in a bay. The way in is a real gate: a lit kiosk with a guard sitting behind the glass, and a leaf that only opens when a member of the night shift badges through it, for about a second and a half. Everything the floor gets a reaction to now waits until the hero is actually through it, so the read on the lit desks and the scripted first blow land inside the building rather than out on the car park.
