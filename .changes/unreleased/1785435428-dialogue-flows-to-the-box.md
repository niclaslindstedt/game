---
type: Changed
title: Dialogue fills the box it is in
---

Every spoken surface — the opening and closing monologues, the in-world dialogue
box, cutscene captions, the merchant and the quest givers — now flows its text
into the width the box actually has instead of printing the author's typed line
breaks, so a page fills a desktop window and folds cleanly on a portrait phone;
the SpaceZ HQ monologue no longer opens by recapping the cutscene it follows.
