title: The hero wears what he did — blood on his gear, his face and his blade
---

Kill things at arm's length and you do not walk away clean. Blood now sprays
back onto the hero and STAYS there, building up over a map from a few flecks to
a horror-film shot: his armor, his boots, his face, and the weapon he did it
with, each soaking independently.

- **A zone is a gear slot.** Swap the breastplate and his front is fresh again
  while the helmet he has worn all level is still crusted; pick a blade up off
  the floor and it comes up clean in his hand. Nothing else ever cleans it —
  there is no decay and he does not wipe it off.
- **It only lands at contact range**, so a build that kills things by walking up
  to them ends up visibly filthier than one that kills at two hundred paces —
  with nothing anywhere reading a weapon's class.
- **The floor marks him back.** Walking through a pool soaks his boots directly
  and splashes up his shins.
- **And his boots track it out.** Bloody footprints print onto clean ground for
  the next few strides and stay there for the rest of the level, exactly as the
  bloodied floor does — a record of where he went rather than of what he did.

All of it hangs off the existing **EXTRA GORE** switch (and the device's MATURE
CONTENT control), so a censored game shows none of it and records none of it.
Two new EFFECTS GALLERY exhibits stage the pair: **DRENCHED** and **BLOODY
BOOTPRINTS**.
