---
type: Changed
title: The moon's dead are people again
---

THE MOON's whole cast was one hump-and-stub-legs template in five colours — a
45hp moon ghost and a 90hp wraith were indistinguishable from the named minions
that guard the detour caches. Every mob now has its own silhouette and the job
it died at, none of them has feet the engine never used, and the regolith's
rocks, its meteor craters and its rare floor tile no longer read as the colour
of the floor behind them.
