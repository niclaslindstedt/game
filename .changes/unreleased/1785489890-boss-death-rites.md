---
type: Added
title: Boss death rites
---

Every boss now leaves the field through a scripted cinematic instead of toppling over: it drops to its knees while the horde is held back and time slows to a crawl, the hero closes and finishes it, and only then does it speak its last words over what is left — and THE FOUNDER, who never dies, tears his rift open, bolts for it and is spun out of existence. SETTINGS → GAMEPLAY → DEATH SCENES turns the finishers (and the hero's own death tableau) off.
