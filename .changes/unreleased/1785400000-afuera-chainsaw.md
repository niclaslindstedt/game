title: The AFUERA CHAINSAW — twenty bodies, then it needs a workshop
---

ELON MOSQUE drops the outsized chainsaw a southern president handed him on a
stage — a two-hander, and the only weapon in the game that does not kill by
damage. Anything you are TOUCHING comes apart on the first bite, at six times
whatever that body was holding, past its armour and past the crit roll, whatever
level it is. It is not swung, either: there is no arc and no slash to read, just
the thing juddering in your hands and what comes off whatever is against you.

Touching is the whole rule. Its reach only says who gets HIT; contact says who
gets taken, so the only way to use it is to walk into the press and lean on it.

It is held back by three things and none of them is a damage number. It has
**twenty teeth and spends one per BODY**, so it takes exactly twenty and is then
scrap in your hand until a repair kit or the merchant sees it. Its **reach and
arc never grow** — MIGHT drives every other melee weapon deeper and INTELLECT
reads every other one wider, and neither does a thing here. And a kill that far
past a body's health pays about a **sixth of its experience and its drop**, so it
clears a room and leaves nothing worth picking up in it.
