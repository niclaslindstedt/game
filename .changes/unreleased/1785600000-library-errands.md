type: Added
title: The library gets an ERRANDS section — every side quest, browsable
---

The reference site at `/library/` now covers the field's side errands: one page
per quest — what it asks for, who is carrying the pieces it wants and at what
odds, what it pays (with the reward's experience priced out per difficulty
rather than left as a share of a level bar), and which errand turning it in
opens — and one page per quest giver, with their whole chain and the paragraph
saying who they are. The index groups both the way the game does, by the venue
they stand on, and every venue's mission page now names the two people on it who
want something other than a fight.

Every errand also carries an authored paragraph of its own now, printed in the
open above its numbers; the conversation itself stays behind a spoiler cover,
like every other spoken line the library publishes.
