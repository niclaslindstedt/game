---
type: Added
title: Ammunition
---

Ranged weapons now run on ammunition instead of wearing out: bullets for every
firearm, arrows for bows, cells for charged shot, each stacking 200 deep in its
own pouch. A run opens with a hundred rounds, the ring around the weapon circle
counts what is left, and supply crates, weapons lockers and cartridge crates are
scattered across the campaign to keep you stocked.
