---
type: Changed
title: Medkits stop raining once your pouch is full
---

The drop ladder now asks how stocked you are before paying out a medkit,
repair kit, or energy drink: a kit the full pouch would refuse is never
dropped, so a healthy hero with five medkits stops finding a sixth on the
floor. Medkits also drop about half as often at baseline and energy drinks
twice as often, so stamina is a resource you actually find.
