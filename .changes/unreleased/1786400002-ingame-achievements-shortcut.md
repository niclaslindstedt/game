type: Added
title: The achievements shelf, mid-run
---

The trophy shelf no longer lives only on the title menu: press **Y** during a
run — World of Warcraft's own achievements key, and rebindable like every other
control under SETTINGS → CONTROLS → KEY BINDINGS — and the shelf rises over the
frozen field, with the run (and its music) paused behind it until you back out
with Y or ESC. Tapping the gold unlock banner when a badge drops opens the same
shelf, so a phone can reach it too; raising the shelf from the pause menu
leaves that menu standing when you close it.
