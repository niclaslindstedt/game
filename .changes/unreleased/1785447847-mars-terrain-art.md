---
type: Changed
title: Mars looks like Mars
---

MARS's terrain art is redrawn: the regolith and its gravel patches no longer
tile into a visible lattice, the boulders are broken wind-scoured stone instead
of smooth potatoes, the craters are dusty Martian scars rather than the moon's
bright-rimmed ones, and the rover tracks and stone scatter are actually visible
on the dust.
