title: The second arm — shields, bigger bags, and two-handed weapons
breaking: true
---

The bag slot is now the OFF HAND, and it holds a choice: a SHIELD (a fifth
armour piece, behind a STRENGTH floor only a melee build clears), a BAG (no
armour, DEX/INT, and cells that grow with a find's item level), or nothing at
all — because the weapon in your other hand is TWO-HANDED. Eleven shields, eight
new bags and five two-handed melee weapons join the catalogs, ten shipped
weapons become two-handers forged 40% over the one-handed damage line, and
eighteen named relics hang off the new bases. All three show on the hero, take
their own blood, and a two-hander is swung around the body rather than off one
shoulder.
