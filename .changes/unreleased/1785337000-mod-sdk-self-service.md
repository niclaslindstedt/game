title: The mod SDK scaffolds, searches and explains itself
---

`node mod/tools/cli.mjs new my-mod` copies the worked example into a mod of your
own with every id renamed, and checks it compiles before saying so — you start
from something that already runs. `ids` searches every id a mod may reference,
and `where` prints the folder that makes the game list your mod. The
step-by-step, from scaffold to publishing on the Workshop, is `mod/AGENTS.md`.
