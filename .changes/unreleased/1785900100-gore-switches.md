title: A GORE page, one switch per kind
---

SETTINGS → VIDEO → GORE replaces the single EXTRA GORE switch with eight: one
per kind of body (HUMAN, GHOST, ROBOTIC and COSMIC GORE), one for each way a body
comes apart (CLEAVES, GIBS), and one each for the blood that stays on your gear
and the trail your boots track out of it — so turning off human gore leaves the
machines sparking. A save that had EXTRA GORE off keeps all of it off.
