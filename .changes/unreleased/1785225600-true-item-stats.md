---
type: Changed
title: Weapon numbers mean what they say
---

A weapon's damage and attack speed are now exactly the numbers on its card: the hidden halving that cut every looted weapon to half its listed damage is gone, weapons no longer gain damage from item level (item level buys the affix budget instead), and the global cadence penalty that made every weapon swing slower than it read is gone too. Monster health carries the difficulty instead, doubled to match, so a monster still takes about the same number of hits to kill as before.
