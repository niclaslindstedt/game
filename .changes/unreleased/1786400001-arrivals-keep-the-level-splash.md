---
type: Fixed
title: Arrivals keep the level splash
---

Arriving at a level whose opening has already been watched — driving out of
the garage, retrying after a death — now lands on the level-name card instead
of dropping straight into combat with no announcement (the cutscene, briefing
and scripted opening strike stay skipped). Runs that never showed their
opening (a developer warp, a staged scenario, a muted run) no longer mark it
as watched, so a level's real first visit still tells its story. The card also
auto-advances reliably while a busy screen renders behind it.
