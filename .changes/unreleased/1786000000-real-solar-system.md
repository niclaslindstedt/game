type: Changed
title: A real solar system on the title screen
---

The main menu's backdrop is now the whole solar system, on the real numbers.
All eight planets share one ecliptic and run on their true orbital elements, so
the orbits are real ellipses with the sun at a focus. Every world wears its own
geography — Earth has an actual world map with a cloud layer drifting over it,
Venus its super-rotating deck, Mars its dark markings and Valles Marineris, the
Moon its near-side maria, Saturn its rings with the Cassini division — and the
airless worlds have no atmospheric glow, because they have no atmosphere.
