title: Mars is flown to from your own lawn
---

The trip to Mars used to open on the moon — a suited hero saying goodbye to a ghost on regolith — even though you fly there from the garage, having walked home and boarded the ship yourself. It opens on the launch now, from the lot, with the house you burned the first time already burnt beside the pad. THE FLAGBEARER's send-off plays where it actually happens: the moment you win the flag, before the moon lets you go.
