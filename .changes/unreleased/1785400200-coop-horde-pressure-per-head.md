---
type: Fixed
title: Co-op — walking together no longer streams in a horde per player
---

"Walking stirs the horde" banked its pressure once per hero into one counter
shared by the run, so a party of eight travelling together summoned reinforcements
eight times as fast as one person covering the same ground. It is now measured
per head, which is what the rule always meant: a group walking down a corridor
has walked down one corridor.

Single player is unaffected.
