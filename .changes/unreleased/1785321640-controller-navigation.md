type: Added
title: Play the whole game with a controller
---

A gamepad now drives every menu in the game, not just the run: the title screen and its settings, the pause menu, your inventory and the shop, the level-up and talent choosers, the vault, the achievement shelf and the high-score board. The stick or d-pad moves, A chooses, B and START back out — so a controller is enough from launch to quit, with no keyboard or mouse needed.
