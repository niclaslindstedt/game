title: The library's section nav folds into a menu button on a phone
---

Six section names do not fit across a phone, so the library's nav had been
wrapping into two rows of a header that STICKS — chrome that followed the reader
down every page and took four rows off the top of every screenful before the
first sentence of the page they came for.

On a small screen those six links now sit behind one MENU button, and open as a
panel over the page rather than pushing it down. The landscape reference phone
gets its whole header on a single line; the portrait one drops from four rows of
chrome to two. Nothing changes on a screen wide enough to show the sections as
they were.

It runs no JavaScript, like every other page in the library: it is the same
checkbox the spoiler panels are built on, so it takes keyboard focus, opens on
Space, and still shows all six links to a reader whose stylesheet never arrived.
