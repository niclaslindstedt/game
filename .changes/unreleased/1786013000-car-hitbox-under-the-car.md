title: The car blocks where the car is
---

The garage car's collision chain now lies under the car as it is drawn rather than along the nose the picture never shows, so you can no longer walk through the bonnet, get stopped by open floor half a car away, or hop onto a roof that isn't there — including with the developer camera turned round to isometric, where the whole chain used to stand off the body at the camera's own angle.
