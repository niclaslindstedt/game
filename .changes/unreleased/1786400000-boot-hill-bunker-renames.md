title: Four Boot Hill and bunker names
---

THE SNOW ARCHIVE is now THE CORPUS, THE HONORARY RANK is now THE COURTESY STAR, THE KREMLIN USHANKA is now THE INCUMBENT'S USHANKA, and the bunker's KREMLIN SHADOWS are now CONTINUITY SHADOWS. THE LEAK still watches the town from under the water tower, but he now tells you what he actually did: he ran the watching for a living, published everything it held, and nobody read it but the machine.
