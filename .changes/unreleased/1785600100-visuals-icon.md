type: Changed
title: The VISUALS settings row has an icon of its own
---

SETTINGS → VISUALS was wearing the DISPLAY row's monitor, which read as the two
rows being halves of one thing — and the whole reason they are split is that
they are not: DISPLAY decides what is drawn, VISUALS decides how the picture is
lit. It now has its own sparkle. (Row icons show on touch devices, where there
is no hover for the wisp cursor to ride.)
