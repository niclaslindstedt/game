title: The road to GOODCO is a street now
---

The drive has pavements the crowd stands on, crossings it gathers at, a town on
one building line, sparks and grit and smoke where it hits things, a geared
engine note that climbs and drops with the speedo, a shake that grows with the
ride, and far fewer cars in the way — thinnest on EASY, where the people and the
traffic are also light enough to shrug off.
