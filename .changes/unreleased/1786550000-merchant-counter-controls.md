type: Changed
title: The merchant's counter buys by the number and sells by the pick
---

Buying a pile at the stall is one button now: type how many into the field beside it — a number too high snaps to what the trader can actually hand over — and the button reprices itself before you press it. SELL JUNK takes two presses instead of one: the first ticks every outgrown piece and hands the bag over to picking, so you can add a keeper or spare one, hold (or hover) any piece to read its card, and only then take the sale.
