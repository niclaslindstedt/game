title: The AUTO PILOT keeps playing in a multiplayer session
---

The ride's housekeeping — drawing a banked weapon, wearing an upgrade, keeping a
bag cell open, tidying the bag, waking a downed companion — now travels as a run
command, so a flight whose run is simulating on a session server actually happens
instead of being erased by the next update. Cracking a bottle of salts or
spending a medkit also comes out of the right player's own bag.
