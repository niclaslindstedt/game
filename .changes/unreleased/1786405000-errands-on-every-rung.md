type: Fixed
title: THE SCALE can be finished on every difficulty, and its chest with it
---

RUTH's third errand asked for a scale off THE SCALED ANCESTOR, who only ever walks out of a rampage-opened hellgate on JESUS — so on EASY, MEDIUM, HARD and NIGHTMARE the job could not be finished at all, and with it the KEEPSAKE BOX, the HEIRLOOM CHEST, the STEAMER TRUNK and the DOWRY CHEST were unreachable: the hero had nowhere to KEEP anything below the last rung. The scale now also comes off the two the tribute road runs through — RASPUTIN, who the scaled gods pay to watch it, and BRO OMEGA, who sold them the door and cannot be left standing on a cleared rift. Four other errands pointed at the same rampage-only crop and are now offered only on the rungs that actually hold it: SERVICE AT EIGHT and A FITTING from NIGHTMARE up, THE HOTHOUSE and THE CREW ARE LATE on JESUS.
