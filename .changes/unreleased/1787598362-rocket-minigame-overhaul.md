title: The rocket flight, overhauled

The climb to the moon now flies on real physics (a ship that lightens as its tanks drain, layered winds with a dashboard meter, telemetry pegged at orbital speed) through an edgeless sky where drifting off course meets airliners, drones, birds and skydivers — under a full takeoff plume, with the hero's thoughts in place of dialogue windows, a dashboard that fits portrait phones, and an auto-pilot that can fly the whole trip.
