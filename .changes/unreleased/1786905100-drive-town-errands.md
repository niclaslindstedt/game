The road to GOODCO now has a grocer, a supermarket, a laundromat, an off-licence, a diner, a hardware store, a barber, a pawnbroker, a bank and a self-store on it — and a motel, a walk-up and an apartment complex whose galleried floors you drive past at a hundred and twenty.
