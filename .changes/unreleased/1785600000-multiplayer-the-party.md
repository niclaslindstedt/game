type: Added
title: The party
---

A multiplayer run now carries a party of heroes rather than one: a player who
joins a hosted game is seated with a character of their own, and the horde
answers to the whole group — mobs chase the nearest hero they can see, packs
wake for whoever walks up, a blast bills everybody standing in it, and the run
only ends when the last of you is down. Single player is unchanged.
