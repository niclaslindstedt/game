---
type: Changed
title: Loot is thrown, lands, and shows off — Diablo-style drops
---

Loot no longer appears under the corpse. It **bursts out of the body, arcs
through the air, tumbles, and clatters down** a pace or two away, with a shadow
tracking it on the ground and a puff of dust — in the floor's own colour — where
it hits.

The landing **makes a noise, and what it sounds like is what the thing is made
of**: a blade rings, a gun clacks, a wand knocks, plate clangs, mail jingles,
leather slaps, cloth barely flumps, a ring tinks, glass clinks, a repair kit
clatters. On top of that a magic-or-better find rings its **rarity** — a soft
bell for a magic, a full arpeggio with a swell under it for a legendary, and the
rarest sound in the game for an artifact.

**Rare loot now advertises itself from across the field.** The old four corner
pixels are gone; in their place a ladder of effects, each in the item's own
rarity colour: a magic drop lights a pool on the floor, a rare starts smoking, a
set piece fumes, a **unique stands a beam of light over itself**, a legendary is
orbited by motes, and an artifact pulses a ring out across the ground. Every
named find blooms once as it lands, so the good one in a ten-item spill is
findable without reading ten item names.
