title: Ruth introduces herself before she starts asking
---

The first time you speak to RUTH in the garage she tells you how she got there — the midnight call that never said Ada's name, and why she is not going home to sit by the phone — in a conversation you steer rather than an errand handed to a stranger. Her three errands open from the tap after it, and the first one now picks up where the meeting left off.
