---
type: Fixed
title: The hero no longer shoots at monsters hidden in the fog of war
---

The autonomous weapon used to open fire on anything inside its range, whether or
not the ground it stood on had been uncovered — so a long gun advancing into
unexplored territory would fire off into the blackness at a monster nobody could
see. A monster is now a target only once the fog has lifted from where it
stands, and it stays one for the rest of the run (explored ground never
re-fogs). The same rule holds for the crate the auto-attack smashes when no foe
is in reach, for the conjured powers that pick their own mark, and for
companions — the party never attacks what the player cannot see.

Weapon reach is unchanged: a long gun still outranges a pistol on ground the
hero has walked. Advancing into the dark now means closing until the monster is
in the light, and the autopilot holds its stand-off accordingly instead of
parking beyond where its own shots land.
