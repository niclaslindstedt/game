---
type: Changed
title: Gold picked up together adds up into one number
---

Piles of gold collected within a breath of each other — a boss's six-way
fountain, or the trail left across a cleared fight — now float their **total**
as a single "+N" over the hero and write a single line in the pickup feed,
instead of stacking half a dozen identical numbers on the same spot where only
one of them was readable. The same grouping a pack kill's XP already gets.

Every floating status text now takes a row of its own instead of being drawn
into another's pixels: the hit numbers, the XP, the gold, DODGE/PARRY/SHIELDED,
"LEVEL UP!", a companion's banter and a boss's bark all stack into a readable
ladder when they land on the same spot at the same instant.
