type: Changed
title: A new soundtrack
---

Every score in the game has been rewritten, and each one is now built on two or three decisions rather than a mood: GOODCO's floor SINKS a semitone halfway through every phrase while a photocopier clacks against the bar; the moon holds one unmoving note while the harmony re-colours it; Mars sells itself over a landlords' drum that never agrees with the beat; the rift has no tonic, no cadence and a bar that limps; and the garage borrows one minor chord it never puts back. THE BUNKER has its own theme at last — a passacaglia whose ground bass cannot leave the room — instead of borrowing GOODCO's. The title theme is the one you know, with its pads re-struck so they hold and its tune lifted clear of the accompaniment.
