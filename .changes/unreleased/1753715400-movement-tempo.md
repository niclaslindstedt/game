---
type: Changed
title: The world moves half again as fast
---

The hero runs at 84 world px/s rather than 56 — 4.2 body-lengths a second, about
five seconds to cross a phone screen instead of seven and a half — and a winded
hero now jogs at 0.7 of that rather than wading at half. The whole horde was
sped up by the same 1.5 so every chase the fights were tuned on plays out as
before; what changed is the game's tempo, not who wins a footrace. Three new
DEVELOPER → BALANCE sliders — TEMPO, HERO SPEED and MOB SPEED — turn the same
dials at runtime, together or one side at a time.
