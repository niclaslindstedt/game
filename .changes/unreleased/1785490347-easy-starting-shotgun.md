---
type: Changed
title: Grandpa's sawed-off replaces the fire extinguisher
---

EASY now opens with a sawed-off shotgun off the hero's wall instead of a fire
extinguisher: six pellets, its own report, and a point-blank blast that takes a
body apart. Each pellet is priced at about a third of a starting mob's health,
so three landed pellets can kill and the flat damage retires the gun within a
map or two — the wall weapon is the floor any real find supplants.
