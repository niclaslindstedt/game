---
type: Fixed
title: DROP TRASH keeps your backup weapons
---

DROP TRASH no longer destroys the hero's last melee, ranged or magic weapon, and
only parts with a spare he has genuinely climbed past — so a snapped blade or an
empty pouch can never leave him with nothing to fight with. A dry ranged weapon
now also falls back to the built-in sidearm when the bag holds nothing loaded.
