title: He takes the weapon off the wall himself
---

The prelude now SHOWS the hero arming himself: he gets up off the couch, goes
round behind it, leaps for the piece mounted over it, takes it out of the air —
the wall is bare from that frame on — and carries it out the door in his hand.
The level's opening monologue on the far side of the fade shows him holding it
too.
