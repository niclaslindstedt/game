title: A quest's `lore:` warns at 420 chars and the offer pages at 120 — a warning only, so it never fails a build
date: 2026-08-12
scope: content/quests/

`scripts/asset-tools/quest-schema.mjs` carries two separate budgets:
`LORE_WARN_CHARS = 420` for the def's `lore:` paragraph and `PAGE_WARN_CHARS =
120` (with `MAX_PAGE_LINES = 2`) for every spoken page. Both only WARN — the
generator prints `! quest "<id>": lore is N chars` and writes the catalog
anyway, `make test` stays green, and the over-long paragraph ships.

So a lore rewrite needs `npm run levels` and a read of its output, not just a
passing suite. Expanding a lore paragraph to explain a beat is the easy way over
the bar: adding two clauses about who knew what took ruth_receipt from ~420 to
716, and it took two trims to get back under. Say the new fact and cut an old
sentence rather than appending to the paragraph.
