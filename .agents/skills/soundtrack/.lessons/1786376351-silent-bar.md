---
title: A bar with NO note on any voice fails the notation-geometry guard — the engraver has nothing to place, so the sheet's bar spacing doubles
date: 2026-08-10
scope: content/songs/, content/music/
concepts: [notation, authoring, tests, arrangement]
---

`tests/content/notation_geometry_test.ts` measures bar width as the distance
between the first note-head of consecutive bars. A bar that is silent on EVERY
voice contributes no heads at all, so it is skipped and the gap it sits in
measures as two bars — `bars differ in width: expected ['472.000','236.000']`.

An intro that opens with "one ping, then nothing, then the engine" walks
straight into it, which is how AN HOUR BEHIND's intro failed on the first
build. The fix is musical rather than a test change: bring one voice in — a hat
under the empty bar is enough, and usually better anyway, because a completely
dead bar in an intro is rarely the intent.

The rule is only about a bar that is empty ACROSS THE WHOLE SCORE. A voice
resting for a bar, or eight, is fine and is what `hour_behind`'s whole A section
is made of.
