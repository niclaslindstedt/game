---
title: For the one thing a player deliberately AIMS at, a guaranteed picture beats a force ladder
date: 2026-08-08
scope: engine/game/drive/
concepts: [drive, head-on, thresholds, feedback, staging]
---

A force ladder is the right shape for what merely HAPPENS to a player — it is
what makes a road's collisions vary. It is the wrong shape for the one act a
player commits to on purpose. The head-on is the drive's: a player lines the
wagon up and takes it, and a ladder made the result come out differently every
time, so it was never something he could decide to DO.

The rule is two facts and a fixed outcome: SQUARE and CLOSING (`Impact.approach`,
carried on the impact rather than re-derived) skips the ladder and takes the top
rung every time — the driver's upper half and whatever was inside him, out
through the windscreen, on every single one. Nothing is rolled and nothing is
scaled.

Generalizes past the road: when a player can AIM at an outcome, the outcome has
to be repeatable enough to be aimed at. Variety belongs to what he did not
choose.
