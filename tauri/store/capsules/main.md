# Main capsule description

## Scene

Four stocky astronauts make a cooperative stand on cratered lunar regolith as
a varied haunted horde closes in. Earth hangs above the horizon, Ada remains a
small destination beyond the enemy line, and The Flagbearer watches from the
dark background. The real mint wordmark sits across the calm lower ground.

## Visible content

- **Four of up to eight heroes — shipped:** the Steam build supports
  eight-player co-op; four are shown to keep the squad composition readable.
  The mismatched recovered EVA suits are a conceptual rendering of player
  heroes.
- **Ada — symbolic narrative cue:** a tiny unarmed figure in a red jacket,
  white shirt, and blue jeans, separate from the party.
- **Common Moon mobs — shipped:** Moon ghosts, pale wisps, lost cosmonauts,
  purple wraiths, a prospector, and a quarantine medic.
- **Elite — shipped:** Selene Drowned in its antique brass diving helmet.
- **Boss — shipped:** The Flagbearer lurking at the horizon beside its flag.
- **Left weapon — shipped:** Surplus Carbine, rendered as a weathered rifle
  with a compact amber muzzle flash.
- **Middle-left weapon — shipped category:** Scrap Greatsword, represented by
  a broad industrial two-handed blade and its pale melee sweep.
- **Middle-right weapon/effect — shipped:** Pulsar Rod held as a conductor;
  the vertical lightning is the separate Storm Call talent, not a projectile
  fired by the rod.
- **Right weapon — shipped:** Blunderbuss, rendered as a battered wide-bore
  shotgun with a compact muzzle flash.
- **Loot — shipped:** equipment silhouettes under narrow amber rarity beams.
- **Gore/effects — shipped:** pale mint ectoplasm, one cleave, Storm Call, and
  compact muzzle flashes. Ghosts do not bleed red.
- **Environment — shipped/conceptual key art:** lunar grit, craters, mission
  debris, starfield, and distant Earth.

## Crop and text

Exact raster: 1232×706. The party and horde remain inside the central crop.
The checked-in PNG contains the deterministically composited game-font
wordmark; no generated lettering is used.

## Promise audit

Eight-player Steam co-op, the Moon roster, elite and boss, weapons, Storm Call,
ectoplasm, loot, and Earth all exist in shipped gameplay. Four heroes are shown
for visual clarity, not as a player cap. The capsule heightens scale, squad
posing, and simultaneous enemy variety, and uses Ada only as a symbolic
objective. The two center silhouettes are interpretations of shipped content—not
promises of new weapon mechanics.
