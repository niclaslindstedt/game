# Library hero description

## Scene

An ultra-wide lunar panorama places the small hero on a pale ectoplasm route
toward Ada under Earth. Sparse Moon spirits and Selene Drowned occupy the
middle distance while The Flagbearer watches from the black horizon.

## Visible content

- **Hero — shipped:** small EVA-suited player silhouette in the central safe
  area.
- **Ada — symbolic narrative cue:** distant red-jacketed destination.
- **Common Moon mobs — shipped:** sparse ghosts, wisps, lost cosmonauts, and a
  purple wraith.
- **Elite — shipped:** Selene Drowned.
- **Boss — shipped:** The Flagbearer, deliberately secondary.
- **Weapons/items:** no invented weapon is foregrounded; scattered debris is
  non-specific lunar set dressing.
- **Gore/effects — shipped/symbolic:** pale mint ectoplasm trail and wisps.
- **Environment — shipped/conceptual key art:** broad lunar plain, regolith,
  rocks, starfield, and Earth.

## Crop and text

Exact raster: 3840×1240. All narrative subjects live in the center third so
Steam's narrower library crops may discard either flank safely. No text is
baked in; Steam overlays `library-logo.png`.

## Promise audit

The cast, roster hierarchy, ectoplasm, Moon, and Earth are shipped. Their
coexistence and the long continuous path are compressed symbolic storytelling.
The panorama is intentionally quieter than a combat screenshot and does not
promise rocket-riding, bunker play, or a second moon.
