---
title: The ward that keeps the horde off a quest giver is an invisible WALL — keep it small
date: 2026-07-31
---

`QUESTS.repelRadius` doesn't merely stop the horde SPAWNING near a giver, it
pushes minions out and holds them at its edge — so a generous one is a bubble
the player can stand in untouched, parked on top of somebody who stands on
every map in the game.

Shipped first at 96 px, it broke two unrelated content suites
(`tests/content/last_stand_test.ts`, `opening_strike_test.ts`) by shoving
scripted mobs out of the spots their scenes place them in. It is **44** now,
just past the merchant's 40, and that is the ceiling: two givers per map times
six maps is a lot of floor to make unusable. If a giver keeps getting mobbed,
move the giver (or place them off the intended route) rather than widening the
ward.
