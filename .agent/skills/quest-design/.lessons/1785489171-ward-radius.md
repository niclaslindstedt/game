---
title: A quest giver has NO ward — the horde reaches the hero standing on one
date: 2026-08-06
scope: content/quest-givers.yaml
concepts: [quest-giver, ward, horde]
---

Givers used to carry the merchant's ward (`QUESTS.repelRadius`, 44 px): minions
that strayed inside were pushed back out and held at its edge. That is gone.

The reason it went is the reason it was already capped at 44: a bubble the horde
cannot enter is a bubble the PLAYER can stand in untouched, parked on top of
somebody who stands on every map in the game — a free farming spot, twice per
venue. Widening it was never the risk; having it at all was. (Its first shipped
size, 96 px, also broke two unrelated content suites —
`tests/content/last_stand_test.ts`, `opening_strike_test.ts` — by shoving
scripted mobs out of the spots their scenes place them in.)

What survives: givers are still **invulnerable**, so an errand can never be lost
to the horde. What changed: accepting one is done in whatever fight is going on.
So place a giver where a conversation is *possible* — off a wave lane, near the
route rather than in the middle of it — because there is no longer any geometry
protecting that spot. `QUESTS.displaceStep` (22) is all that is left of the old
number: the per-ring step a giver or a quest spot walks when a carved map puts a
wall under its authored coordinates.
