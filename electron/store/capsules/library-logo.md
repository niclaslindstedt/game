# Library logo description

## Asset

The mint `ADA'S TRAIL` wordmark, drawn deterministically from the game's own
pixel-font glyph atlas with the title-screen shadow treatment on a transparent
canvas. It contains no generated lettering, characters, mobs, weapons, items,
effects, or scenery.

## Crop and use

Exact raster: 1280×720 RGBA. Steam overlays it on `library-hero.png`; generous
transparency lets the client position and scale it without a visible box.

## Promise audit

This is the shipped title treatment reproduced from source assets. It makes no
gameplay claim and cannot introduce an image-model spelling or typography
error.
