# Library capsule description

## Scene

A close portrait of the hero on the Moon, Moon's Blade held ready beneath a
weathered EVA helmet. Earth is reflected in the amber visor while pale spirits
and a distant Ada trail recede into the lunar dark; The Flagbearer is barely
visible behind them.

## Visible content

- **Hero — shipped:** ordinary, stocky worker in recovered white-and-grey EVA
  gear.
- **Ada — symbolic narrative cue:** a distant trace/figure beyond the hero.
- **Common Moon mobs — shipped:** varied ghost, wisp, lost-cosmonaut, and wraith
  silhouettes.
- **Boss — shipped:** The Flagbearer as a low-contrast background threat.
- **Weapon — shipped:** Moon's Blade, shown as the curved knife.
- **Gore/effects — shipped:** pale mint ectoplasm and trail wisps; no red ghost
  blood.
- **Environment — shipped/conceptual key art:** cratered regolith, stars, and
  Earth, including the physically coherent visor reflection.

## Crop and text

Exact raster: 600×900. The helmet and blade survive the library-grid crop. The
checked-in PNG contains the deterministically composited game-font wordmark at
the bottom.

## Promise audit

Every identifiable character, enemy family, weapon, landmark, and gore color
comes from the shipped Moon experience. Portrait scale, the visor reflection,
and the compressed background cast are heightened for owned-game library art;
the image does not imply a red moon above the Moon or make Ada playable.
