# Vertical capsule description

## Scene

The helmeted hero follows a luminous ectoplasm trail up a narrow lunar ridge
toward Ada. Earth hangs in the black sky; Moon spirits close along the trail,
Selene Drowned guards the ascent, and The Flagbearer lurks at the edge of the
background.

## Visible content

- **Hero — shipped:** stocky working EVA suit with amber visor and red chest
  indicator.
- **Ada — symbolic narrative cue:** the distant red-jacketed figure at the
  trail's end, not a playable combat companion.
- **Common Moon mobs — shipped:** ghosts, wisps, a lost cosmonaut, and a purple
  wraith.
- **Elite — shipped:** Selene Drowned in the brass diving helmet.
- **Boss — shipped:** The Flagbearer concealed in the upper shadow.
- **Weapon — shipped:** Moon's Blade, the curved lunar knife in the hero's
  hand.
- **Gore/effects — shipped:** the pale mint trail is ghost ectoplasm and
  narrative wayfinding; no red blood or shooting-star attack is shown.
- **Environment — shipped/conceptual key art:** lunar ridge, regolith, stars,
  rocks, and Earth.

## Crop and text

Exact raster: 748×896. The vertical trail keeps hero, Ada, elite, and boss in
the central sale-page crop. The checked-in PNG contains the deterministically
composited game-font wordmark at the bottom.

## Promise audit

The Moon, Earth, Moon's Blade, enemy families, elite, boss, ectoplasm, and the
search for Ada are shipped. The continuous glowing trail and the simultaneous
roster are heightened symbolic key art; they communicate pursuit and haunted
combat without claiming a literal escort mission or a projectile system the
game lacks.
