# Header capsule description

## Scene

A stocky astronaut in a battered white-and-grey working EVA suit cuts a broad
blade through a varied horde on cold lunar regolith. Distant Earth hangs in the
upper-right sky. The left 42% is calm near-black space reserved for the real
wordmark.

## Visible content

- **Hero — shipped:** the player character in the recovered working EVA suit,
  with an amber visor and small red chest indicator.
- **Ada — symbolic narrative cue:** a tiny distant figure in her red jacket,
  white shirt, and blue jeans, beyond the horde as the destination of the trail;
  she is not depicted as a combat companion.
- **Common Moon mobs — shipped:** Moon ghosts, pale wisps, lost cosmonauts,
  purple wraiths, prospectors, and a quarantine medic.
- **Elite — shipped:** Selene Drowned, identifiable by its antique brass diving
  helmet and green porthole.
- **Boss — shipped:** The Flagbearer lurking in the upper background beside a
  dark planted American lunar flag.
- **Weapon — shipped category, conceptual rendering:** a broad melee blade
  producing the game's pale cleave arc; the image does not assert a specific
  item id.
- **Loot — shipped:** small equipment silhouettes with narrow amber rarity
  beams.
- **Gore/effects — shipped:** pale mint ectoplasm splashes, smears, and wisps;
  no red blood. One pale melee cleave arc is the sole attack effect.
- **Environment — shipped/conceptual key art:** grey regolith, grit, pocks,
  shallow pits, low rocks, stars, and distant Earth.

## Crop and text

Exact raster: 920×430. Keep the combat tableau on the right and composite the
real mint wordmark into the empty left field. The checked-in PNG contains the
deterministically composited game-font wordmark; no generated lettering is
used.

## Promise audit

The Moon biome, top-down horde pressure, melee cleave, ectoplasm, loot beams,
common/elite/boss hierarchy, Earth, Ada, and every named enemy are shipped.
The poster enlarges silhouettes, compresses several Moon encounters into one
tableau, and shows Ada as a symbolic distant destination rather than a field
companion. It does not claim a second planet, red lunar reflection, shooting
stars, or an unshipped weapon/effect.
