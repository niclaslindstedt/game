# Store page background description

## Scene

A restrained near-black starfield with a faint grey lunar edge and a barely
visible mint ectoplasm trace. It is atmospheric support for the Steam page,
not a standalone key-art scene.

## Visible content

- **Characters, mobs, bosses, weapons, and items:** none.
- **Effect — shipped/symbolic:** very faint pale mint ectoplasm.
- **Environment — shipped/conceptual:** black space, sparse stars, and lunar
  regolith at the edge.

## Crop and text

Exact raster: 1438×810. Contrast is intentionally low so Steam page copy,
screenshots, and purchase controls remain legible. No text is baked in.

## Promise audit

The starfield, Moon, and ectoplasm palette are truthful to the game. The image
makes no mechanical or narrative promise and avoids a red moon, rocket, bunker,
or invented prop.
